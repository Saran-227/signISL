Stage 01 · Ingestion Layer

Multimodal Acoustic & Text Ingestion

Continuous acoustic capture across 12+ Indian regional dialects (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, Marathi, and more) plus live WebRTC audio streams, web DOM text selection, and clinical dictation feeds.

WebRTC Audio Stream Noise-Robust ASR 12+ Indian Languages DOM Text Hook

02
Stage 02 · NLP Engine

Multilingual Semantic & Intent Parsing

Bidirectional neural transformer parses complex compound sentences, handling medical terminology, railway announcements, and colloquial regional idioms. Extracts core semantic predicates and intent without lexical loss.

Bidirectional Attention Domain Tokenizer Colloquial Normalization Context Windowing
Semantic Extraction: <40ms
03
Stage 03 · ISL Transformer

Spatial ISL Grammar & Gloss Translation

Unlike spoken languages, Indian Sign Language uses a non-linear spatial grammar with Topic-Comment ordering and directional non-manual markers. Our proprietary transformer maps semantics directly into verified ISL Gloss taxonomy.

Topic-Comment Syntax Spatial Grammar Matrix 10,000+ Sign Database ISLRTC Compliant
Gloss Translation: <45ms
04
Stage 04 · Kinematic Rigging

Kinematic Interpolation & 3D Motion Synthesis

Transforms discrete sign gloss tokens into continuous 60fps skeletal joint rotations (27 hand bones, wrists, elbows, and shoulder pivots). Integrates smooth co-articulation easing and realistic facial expression markers.

27-Bone Hand Rig Co-Articulation Easing Facial Grammar Rig 60 FPS Kinematics
Kinematic Synthesis: <48ms
05
Stage 05 · Rendering & Delivery

Zero-Plugin WebGL Multi-Surface Distribution

Lightweight client-side WebGL / WebGPU avatar engine that runs seamlessly across any browser, low-spec Android devices, hospital bedside tablets, and public railway LED arrival displays without installing apps or plugins.

WebGL 2.0 / WebGPU Zero Plugins Cross-Platform Kiosk & Display Ready
Render Frame Budget: 16.6ms (60 FPS)
06
Stage 06 · Linguistic Validation

Continuous Verification with Certified Deaf Educators

Continuous human-in-the-loop validation pipeline connected to certified Indian Sign Language teachers, deaf schools, and accessibility advocates across India to ensure regional vocabulary integrity, cultural respect, and grammatical precision.
